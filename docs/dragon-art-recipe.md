# Individual Dragon Art Recipe

## Output

- Generate one image per species and evolution stage.
- Output: `1024x1024` PNG.
- Format: RGBA with transparency.
- Filename: `public/assets/dragons/dragon-<three-digit-dex-number>.png`
- Examples:
  - `public/assets/dragons/dragon-001.png`
  - `public/assets/dragons/dragon-002.png`
  - `public/assets/dragons/dragon-284.png`

## Generation requirements

1. Generate the species and evolution stage independently.
2. Use a full-body, centered front or three-quarter view.
3. Match the approved clean 2D fantasy-dragon style used for the approved individual art.
4. Make each evolution visibly distinct and stronger:
   - Stage 1: smallest and cutest.
   - Stage 2: larger and more detailed.
   - Stage 3: largest, broadest, and most powerful.
5. Match the dragon's type:
   - Fire: orange, red, gold
   - Water: blue
   - Grass: green
   - Ice: pale blue, cyan, white
   - Electric: yellow, gold, bright accent
   - Light: cream, pearl, gold
   - Cloud: pale blue, white, soft fluff
   - Dark: purple, indigo, violet
6. Include no text, logo, watermark, border, extra character, repeated pose, or separate UI.
7. Use the transparency already generated by the image model. Do not remove, recolor, resize, filter, crop, or otherwise transform the approved PNG afterward.

## Reference inputs

For a batch, provide:

- This species' exact name and Dex number.
- Its evolution chain and stage.
- Its type and major identifying features.
- The approved individual art as a style reference.
- The roster sheet only as a consistency reference.

Do not use another dragon's cutout or an automatic crop of the roster sheet as the final image. Roster cells are not individual generated artwork.

## Integration

`src/App.tsx` loads battle and Dex images from:

```text
/assets/dragons/dragon-<three-digit-dex-number>.png
```

No species-specific rendering exception is needed for a completed dragon. The existing `DragonIcon` component automatically loads the matching direct PNG in Dex details, battles, captures, evolutions, and starter selections.

## Approval checklist

- File is exactly `1024x1024`.
- File is RGBA PNG.
- Original generated alpha/transparency is preserved.
- The dragon is a full body.
- The dragon is centered and readable at Dex, battle, and capture sizes.
- The species and type are correct.
- Its evolution is visually larger and distinct.
- No manual transformations or species exceptions were added.
